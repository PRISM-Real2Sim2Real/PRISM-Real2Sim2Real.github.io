02 THE IDEA & THE METHOD

New objects.
New ways
to interact.

Starting from a few real videos, PRISM generates new objects together with the human motions to match.

We reconstruct these interactions in 3D and train in simulation, so one humanoid policy can pick, carry, and drop unfamiliar objects in the real world.

04 THE TAKEAWAY

Sample interactions.
Ground them in physics.
Transfer the skill.

PRISM uses the diversity of generated human–object interactions to train a single humanoid policy for pick, carry, and drop. The aim is not simply to imitate more videos—it is to acquire a skill that adapts to the next object.

About the demonstrations and release materials

The demonstrations use one policy with autonomous low-level control and joystick steering, rather than fully autonomous task planning. Generated videos supply visual interaction priors; reconstruction and simulation provide physical grounding. Paper and implementation links will accompany the public research release.
