REAL ROBOT · ZERO-SHOT TRANSFER

Teaching robots to pick up different objects

Humanoids can walk. Now they need to handle everyday objects.

A useful household robot must grasp a ball, lift a bucket, and approach a box from the side. New shapes, sizes, and poses demand new movements.

People learn these adjustments through years of watching and practice. How do we give robots that experience?

02 / The data bottleneck

Finding useful demonstrations

Show a robot how a person moves. VideoMimic turns human video into skills such as climbing and sitting. To scale this approach, we need useful demonstrations.

03 / Our solution

Generate more interactions from one video

We start with a real demonstration. Video-to-video generation (V2V) changes the object and adapts the person’s motion.

THE RECORDED EXAMPLECOUNTERFACTUAL POSSIBILITIES
Vary the object. Adapt the interaction.
COUNTERFACTUAL /

A counterfactual video shows an interaction that didn’t happen, but could have with a different object.

We vary object shape, size, and pose, along with the human motion. Scene changes offer a future extension.

Why use video instead of text?

Text describes an event. Video shows the scene, person, and task. V2V varies the interaction; we refine the motion for physical feasibility.

Our seed recordings yield 256 counterfactual videos for training.

05 / From video to robot

Turn videos into robot skills

We reconstruct each video in 3D, adapt the motion to the robot, and train in simulation. Generation and reconstruction can introduce errors, so we refine the trajectories for physical feasibility.

We preserve contact throughout: where the hands hold the object and how they move together.

  1. 01

    Reconstruct the interaction

    Recover the person, object, and scene in one 3D frame. Contact anchors keep their motion consistent through occlusions.

  2. 02

    Retarget to the humanoid

    Adapt the motion to the robot’s body. Preserve hand–object contacts and enforce physical constraints.

  3. 03

    Learn in simulation

    Train a teacher to track robot and object motion. Distill its behavior into one policy that uses onboard depth.

  4. 04

    Transfer to the real world

    Deploy without real-world fine-tuning. The robot picks up, carries, and drops unfamiliar objects while a person supplies steering commands.
